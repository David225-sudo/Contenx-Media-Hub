## Contenx Media Hub Sora plan

Goal: create a short landing-page hero video for Contenx Media Hub that communicates growth-focused digital marketing without using real people, copyrighted characters, or platform logos.

Recommended first concept:
- A premium motion-graphics scene built from translucent dashboard panels, search/analytics cues, and a single rising growth line.
- This matches the site's positioning around strategy, SEO, analytics, and ROI while staying within Sora safety constraints.

Why this concept:
- The website copy emphasizes growth, campaigns, SEO, reporting, and measurable ROI.
- The site palette already centers a vivid purple with white, charcoal, and cool neutrals.
- Avoiding people and brand logos keeps the clip safer and more reusable across the landing page.

Generation settings:
- Model: `sora-2-pro`
- Size: `1280x720`
- Seconds: `4`
- Format: silent or low-ambient hero background

Iteration path:
1. `v1` establishes the core composition and motion.
2. `v2` keeps the shot but simplifies the UI shapes and makes the brand palette more premium.
3. `v3` keeps the shot and palette but adds clearer search and ROI storytelling cues without increasing motion.

Expected use:
- Website hero background
- Social teaser cutdown
- Header video in proposals or pitch decks
